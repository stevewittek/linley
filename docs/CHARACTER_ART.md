## Final Lorelei prompt

Use case: identity-preserve. Asset type: single original 2D game character sprite on genuinely transparent background. Use only the older girl on the RIGHT wearing a BLACK shirt as identity reference; her parent identifies her as Lorelei. Make ONE full-body, standing, front-facing friendly illustrated adventure-game avatar clearly inspired by her actual face: fair/light skin, blue-gray eyes, light brown/dark blonde hair parted near center and tied back with small loose side strands, natural sweet smile and facial proportions. Keep her a child of the same apparent age; do not age her up or infantilize her. Outfit inspired by reference: plain black short sleeve T-shirt with one small original silver star, denim shorts, white sneakers, optional subtle purple wristband. No copied band photos, KATSEYE text or other shirt trademarks. Polished crisp 2D hand-painted/vector-like game art with soft dimensional shading, appealing age10–13 adventure aesthetic, simple clean silhouette legible at 64px, head slightly enlarged for game readability but not babyish. Arms relaxed at sides, feet apart a little, full shoes visible, entire body centered filling canvas with small transparent margin. No scene, ground, text, props, shadows outside figure, other child, labels, UI, sprite sheets or multiple poses. This is a stylized child game avatar, not a photorealistic person.
