# Linley 0.3 product notes

Linley is Lorelei's iPad-first creative app, managed under Netherwood Data Partners. The product should feel simple and fun for ages 10 and 11 while remaining usable by anyone. Lorelei leads the ideas; Dad approves all product, code, account, publishing, and sharing decisions.

## Product rules

- Creator-led: Lorelei and her testers decide what is fun and understandable.
- Dad-approved: a child's suggestion never changes the real app automatically.
- Local-first: creations and feature cards stay on the device unless a parent deliberately exports them.
- Safety-first: no direct messaging, stranger discovery, public posting, location sharing, or open comments.
- Minimal identity: a nickname and built-in avatar are enough for this prototype.
- iPad-first: tablet layouts are intentional and still adapt cleanly to iPhone.
- Free-first: avoid paid infrastructure until a real need and a parent-approved plan exist.

## Current visual direction

Linley uses coral, blush, and white; rounded cards; a prominent script-style `L`; and friendly, readable controls. The brand should feel energetic and polished without copying another artist's identity.

## Version 0.3 boundaries

The Idea Lab is a curated collection of prompts, not a public feed. The video creator records and previews short clips locally, with no upload button. Builder suggestions are stored locally and marked **Dad review**. Sharing a suggestion uses the iPad's standard share sheet and does not provide code access.

## Deliberately absent

- Public profiles, followers, friend requests, or searchable children
- Direct messaging, open chat, or unrestricted comments
- Uploading, cloud video hosting, or recommendation algorithms
- Remote app editing, credentials, repositories, or developer access for children
- Analytics, advertising, behavioral tracking, or third-party social SDKs

Future sharing requires a separate parent-controlled design, privacy review, and explicit Dad approval before implementation.
